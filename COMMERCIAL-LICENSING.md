## No automatic commercial grant

The public repository does not grant commercial-use rights beyond rights that may independently exist under law or a separate written agreement.

If a planned use is connected to commercial products, paid services, revenue-generating activity, business operations or an anticipated commercial application, and you are uncertain whether it is permitted, obtain a separate written license before relying on NubiSync for that use.

## Separate commercial licenses

A rights holder may choose to offer a separate commercial license on individually negotiated terms.

No commercial license is created by:

- opening an issue
- submitting a pull request
- downloading a release
- receiving technical assistance
- receiving no response to a licensing inquiry

A commercial license exists only when granted in a separate written agreement by a party that actually holds the rights necessary to grant it.

## Third-party components

A NubiSync commercial license cannot override licenses for third-party dependencies or code not owned or relicensable by the commercial licensor.

Anyone distributing NubiSync is responsible for complying with all applicable third-party notices and licenses.

## Contributions and relicensing

External contributions can create additional copyright ownership. Before NubiSync accepts substantive third-party code intended to remain eligible for commercial relicensing, the project must adopt an appropriate contributor agreement or another legally sufficient rights arrangement.

Until that mechanism is adopted, project maintainers should avoid merging substantive external code that would create ambiguity about future commercial licensing rights.

Commercial permission for software does not automatically grant rights to use the NubiSync name, logo or other project branding. See `TRADEMARKS.md`.
