# Sponsoring NubiSync

NubiSync may accept voluntary sponsorships and donations to support continued development, testing, infrastructure, documentation and maintenance.

Sponsorship is support for the project and its maintainers.

A sponsorship or donation does **not**:

- purchase ownership of NubiSync or any part of the project;
- transfer copyright, trademark or other intellectual-property rights;
- grant a commercial software license;
- modify the terms of the NubiSync software license;
- grant exclusivity;
- create a partnership, employment, agency or ownership relationship;
- guarantee implementation of a requested feature;
- guarantee support, service levels or maintenance;
- grant the right to present a product, fork or service as an official NubiSync release.

Any benefits explicitly associated with a GitHub Sponsors tier are limited to the benefits stated for that tier.

Commercial software rights, enterprise support, integration services or other commercial arrangements require a separate written agreement with the applicable rights holder.

Individuals and organizations are welcome to sponsor NubiSync regardless of whether they separately qualify to use the software under its license.

**Sponsorship helps NubiSync continue to exist. It does not buy NubiSync.**
